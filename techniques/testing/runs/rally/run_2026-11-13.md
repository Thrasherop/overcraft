# Rally's shockwave, #120 and #122 (fix/rally-shockwave, 2026-11-13)

Up: [Techniques](../../../README.md) · the hero: [brigitte](../../../heroes/brigitte.md) · the rule: [barriers](../../../minecraft/barriers.md) · before: [polish4](../../polish4.md)

**Where it stood:** both were fixed on fix/polish-4 (e299f07, merged into next at 48cd86c) and the issues left open. This branch
re-played them on next 60de166 (0.3.43, after the barrier unification), made the wave's centre explicit
(`Barrier.shields(e, player, centre)`, `Combat.inSight` from the same point) and added `bot fix wave on|off` to switch the sight and
barrier test off in the same build (`bot fix rally` already did the bashed-target exclusion).

**How it was played:** one client, fresh flat worlds (`rally_wave_3`, `_4`), noon, `spawn_monsters false`, me Brigitte on red in hero
mode; Q, 1.2 s, the barrier held (`key primary_fire down`), the bash (`key secondary_fire`), all harness keys (KeyMapping.set), not a
person's hands. Zombies NoAI with fire resistance; the enemy is a `bot` Brigitte on blue 3.8 m past the bashed zombie, facing me,
barrier up by `bot secondary <name> on` or down. Script: [rally_wave.py](rally_wave.py); raw: [rally_wave_run4.txt](rally_wave_run4.txt),
[rally_wave_wall.txt](rally_wave_wall.txt). Minecraft health: 20 = 250, so 70 = 5.6 (14.4 left on a 20 zombie; the bot's 14.96 is
70 less her 50 armor's cut).

- With the check off on today's next, the barrier's hurt-time absorb (`Barrier.absorb`, the unified rule) already takes the wave's
  damage (140: the bot's 70 and the zombie's), but the push and the stun went through. The report on abde763 saw her barrier take
  nothing; the hurt-time rule came later. Fix on: none of it reaches them, and the barrier takes nothing (ours: no source says the
  wave damages a barrier).
- Screenshots (gitignored): `overcraft_rally/run/screenshots/rw_on_barrier_up.png` (Rally 350 hp, the enemy's barrier ahead),
  `rw_off_*`, `rw_on_wall.png`.

**Harness traps found here:**
- **A Rally still running from the case before** (10 s) leaves `/overcraft ult` unable to fill the charge, Q does nothing, and the
  bash lands as the old Rally ends: no wave. Runs 1 to 3 (deleted) read that as "barrier-down bot not hit". The script now waits until
  the charge takes before Q.
- **The open zombie** first stood where the line from her eyes clipped the wall's edge (`sight=false` in a temporary log): moved.
- **The server crashed twice** with `IllegalStateException: Tried to get player before player joined` in
  `ReplayPlayerRecorder.takeSnapshot` (`PotgRecorder.tick`), after several `bot add` / `bot remove`: POTG's recorder and DevBot's bots.
  Not this branch's; reported, not fixed.

**Not tried:** a real second client (what the bot sees); a mouse and keyboard by hand; a Sigma barrier in the wave's way (the shared
`Barriers.between` covers it, unplayed); the wave's shape (`op89(2.25, 8)`: ours stays the half-plane ahead).
