# Tracer: her body's glow (#91, 2026-11-14)

Up: [Tracer](../../../heroes/tracer.md) · the method: [rendering, Glows](../../../rendering/rendering.md#glows-emission-from-the-data-2026-11-04-91)

## The cause, from the data (offline, Overwatch closed)
- Her body 7E12 (shader 0xD9, emission strength 10): emission mask 0xBCBE747D = 20473, a 512 px image of two bands, two discs
  and two thin lines. Laid over her colour image 20470 (TEXCOORD_0) it covers her face, neck and chest: why 0.3.47 left
  `tracer` out of `HERO_IDS` and her accelerator never glowed.
- 7E12's static 0xC7526E90 (io_scene_owm "UVLayer Emission") = 2. TEXCOORD_1 of `tracer.glb` drawn over 20473: the
  accelerator's two discs (front and back) sit on the two circles, its ring on the bands, the legs on the two lines; the rest of
  the body is folded into a black corner.
- The same move tried on every material whose static says 2 or 3 darkened D.Va 98C0/A5D8, Lúcio 801D, Mercy's staff 7FDB and
  Pharah's gun A0AA (4-21% of each image lit before, under 0.5% after). Their UV0 bakes stay; the move is opt-in (`MOVED_UV`).

## The fix
`tools/glow_maps.py`: `owmat` reads the emission UV layer, `glb_uvs` reads both layers and the triangles from the .glb,
`transfer` redraws the mask onto TEXCOORD_0 (each triangle filled, the mask read through the other layer), then the usual
mask x colour. `tracer` back in `HERO_IDS`. The bake (`000000007e12.png`) lights only the accelerator's two blue dots, its blue
bar and the blue lines on her boots. Also new: 7E16, her goggles' lens (shader 0x26, the usual 0.25).

Pack: `OC_ASSET_PACK=E:\claude\mods\overcraft\local-assets\pack`, `py tools/build_asset_pack.py glows` over the 12
`research/<hero>/extract` roots + `extracted/154088`. Every other glow table and image compared byte for byte with a backup
taken before: identical. New: `tracer_glow.json`, `tracer_1p_glow.json`, `glow/000000007e12.png`, `glow/000000007e16.png`.
