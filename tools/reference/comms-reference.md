# Pings, the communication wheel, voice lines, emotes, sprays: what Overwatch 2 does

The owner's spec and screenshots (2026-10-27, Overwatch 2 at 2000x1125, Kiriko; the images themselves are kept out of
the repo, in `local-assets/reference/owner-2026-10-27/`). Text only. Issues: #3 wheel, #4 emotes, #5 voice lines,
and the new ping, spray and climb-reload issues.

## Pings (owner: "single click looks like first image ... double click ... a warning explicitly")
- **A ping** (2.webp): a small **blue diamond** (~22 px, #29C4F5) at the spot aimed at, its distance above it in
  small white type ("21m"), and a thin blue leader line up and to the right to a label **"CANCEL [P]"**: dark blue box,
  white caps, the key in a small light box (the owner's ping key there is P). Seen through walls.
- **Double click: a warning** (3.webp): the marker becomes a **red circle** (~45 px) with a white danger glyph inside
  (a triangle over a skull-like mark), a faint red ring on the ground under it, "22m" above, and the same leader line
  and label in **red**.
- **On something notable** (4.webp, an enemy Tracer): the marker turns **red and shows that hero's portrait** in a
  circle (~55 px, red ring), the distance above ("6m"), the **hero's name in red caps** under it ("TRACER"), and the
  enemy is outlined red. Health packs get their own marker (the owner).
- For Minecraft (ours): enemies are hostile mobs and enemy players (a hero's portrait; a mob: its face or name);
  "notable" also covers what Minecraft has instead of health packs (to decide: dropped items, food, chests).

## The communication wheel (owner; 5.webp)
- **Hold T**: the wheel appears in the middle, the view dimmed. Move the mouse toward an option to hover it; **release
  T** to play it. Released in the centre: nothing. **A quick tap of T** plays "Good job".
- While T is held, **left click: the voice lines page; right click: the emotes page** (tabs on the screen's edges:
  a white bar "VOICE LINES" on the left with a left-mouse icon, "EMOTES" on the right with a right-mouse icon).
- Layout: a white ring ~200 px across at the centre; thin white divider lines out from it between eight options; each
  option a white glyph with a white caps label: top NO (X), top-right HELLO (open hand), right NEED HEALING/HELP
  (plus; a small key box "L" between it and the ring: its own bind), bottom-right GROUP UP, bottom ULTIMATE STATUS
  (key box "Z" above it), bottom-left NEED HELP (warning triangle), left SORRY (face), top-left THANKS (thumbs up).
- What a callout does (owner, 2026-10-26): the chat line and its icon reach teammates (scoreboard team; every player
  when there are no teams) at any distance; the hero's voice line plays in the world to everyone nearby.

## Voice lines page (6.webp)
- Four equipped lines at the four compass points, each in a dark box with a light outline and white caps: top
  "TAROT READING", right "THE SKY WILL CLEAR", bottom "STAY COOL", left "THE DIRTY WORK"; diagonal divider lines;
  the same centre ring. The right edge tab reads "COMMUNICATIONS" (right-mouse icon) to go back.

## Emotes
- All of a hero's emotes, with a settings menu to choose which are equipped on the wheel (owner).
