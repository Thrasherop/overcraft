## Role and sub-role passives
- **Damage role passive: none since BZ 2026-02-10.** Its effect is now global: damage dealt cuts the target's healing received **-30 %**
  (**-15 %** on tanks) for **3 s** (BZ 2026-02-10; W) **H** (W's global page marks it "removed": see the Tracer note, Gap 3 there).
- Global out-of-combat regeneration: after **6 s** without damage, **10 HP/s + 5 % of max HP/s** = **22.5 HP/s** for him (BZ
  2024-05-14, 2025-06-24, 2026-02-10 "6 s remains"; W Global Passive) **H**.
- **Sharpshooter:** **critical hits reduce his movement ability cooldowns** (Combat Roll) by **0.75 % of the damage dealt, in seconds**
  (BZ 2026-02-10 "Cooldown Reduction: 0.75% of damage dealt"; W Sub-Roles text and Template:Sharpshooter value table: Peacekeeper
  headshot `0.0075 * 140` = **1.05 s**) **H**. No later live change (BZ 2026-03 subrole tuning skips it; Hacked 2026-07-30 skips it).
  W's infobox field says "0.75 % of base cooldown" (Gap 2). It replaced Gun Slingin' (W). Whether falloff-reduced crits give less: by
  the "of damage dealt" wording, yes **L**.

## Flashbang (ability 2, E): the live grenade
- **Verdict: Flashbang.** Magnetic Grenade had replaced the old stun Flashbang in Overwatch 2 (before the BZ range read; W
  files it under "Removed Abilities", "Replaced in Season 11 by reworked Flashbang"), and **BZ 2024-06-20 (Season 11) reverted it**: "Magnetic
  Grenade ability is now Flashbang", developer comment "We're reverting the Magnetic Grenade ability to be deployed like Cassidy's
  previous Flashbang ability, but it will keep the Hinder effect instead of a full stun." Every live Cassidy line since names Flashbang
  (2024-09-17, 2025-11-11, Bang Bang 2025-02-18 / 2025-08-26), W's current kit lists Flashbang and files Magnetic Grenade under
  "Removed Abilities". **No live perk restores Magnetic Grenade**: the only "becomes magnetic" is Stadium's Hot Potato power (BZ
  2025-12-09). It is **not the old stun Flashbang**: it hinders. **H**.
- **75 damage** (explosive) (BZ 2024-09-17 45 -> 75; W) **H**. No headshot.
- **Hinder 1.2 s** (BZ 2025-11-11 0.9 -> 1.2; W) **H**: **slow -50 %** (to 2.75 m/s), interrupts and blocks movement abilities, **blocks
  crouching** (BZ 2024-06-20; W) **H**. Cleansable (phased out removes it, e.g. Protection Suzu) (W).
- **Cooldown 12 s** (BZ 2024-06-20; W) **H**.
- **Radius 3 m** (W) **M**. **Max range 7 m, max travel 0.23 s**, projectile **30 m/s** ("needs verification, old value" on W) and
  **radius 0.2 m**, arcing (W) **M**/**L**. 7 m / 0.23 s = 30.4 m/s, so the two agree.
- **Explodes at once** on hitting an enemy or terrain, or at max range; effects to all enemies in the radius (W) **M**. No homing, no
  sticking, no fuse.
- **Cast 0.128 s startup + 0.608 s recovery** (W) **M**.
- In-air projectile can be eaten by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix, Kekkai Sanctuary, Trash Compactor and
  reflected by Deflect; the splash is not affected (W) **M**.
- For the record, Magnetic Grenade (removed, W): 10 stick + 70 -> 18 explosion, 3 m, homing lock-on 1.3 m, 25 m/s thrown / 10 m/s
  homing, 1.25 s fuse once stuck, -25 % slow and hinder, 12 s.

## Deadeye (ultimate, Q)
- **Cost 1800** (W; BZ 2024-09-17 -10 %) **M**.
- **Lock-on: 150 damage per second for the first 2 s, then 300 per second** for the rest (BZ 2024-02-13 130/260 -> 150/300; W) **H**.
  Each target accumulates its own; a red closing circle shows it against the target's health, a skull when lethal (W).
- **Max 7 s**; if it runs out he does **not fire** (W) **M**. Max damage per target 1800 (2 x 150 + 5 x 300) (W) **M**.
- **Movement speed -70 % -> -35 %** (1.65 -> 3.575 m/s) and **damage taken -40 % -> 0 %** (BZ 2024-06-20; W) **H**: after the 2 s
  warning time, the charge rate rises and both fade to their end values over the next **1.4 s** (W) **M**.
- **Fire**: press Ultimate again or Primary Fire; needs at least one target in unobstructed line of sight; one shot per target,
  **1 per 0.112 s**, cast 0.704 s startup + 0.336 s recovery when fired (W) **M**. Shots aim at the chest (W). Range 200 m, a **105°
  3D cone** independent of the FOV setting (W) **M**. Locks only on heroes, B.O.B. and Immortality Field (W).
- **Cancel with Secondary Fire** (0.288 s + 0.4 s) (W) **M**. Stuns, knockdowns and hacks end it without firing (W). No jumping,
  crouching or other abilities while channelling (W). Line of sight lost: that target's charge pauses; invisibility resets it (W).
- **Activating it refills the cylinder** (W); it keeps firing past 6 targets even with no ammo (W).
- **No ult-charge refund in the live game** (Past Noon, 30 -> 40 % on full expiry, removed BZ 2025-04-22) **H**.
- **Cannot attach to Jetpack Cat's Lifeline** once cast (BZ 2026-10-06; W) **H**.
- Voice: the old official description is "Focus. Mark. Draw."; the "It's high noon" call: not in the pages read (Gap 6).

## Disagreements and gaps
1. **Grenade:** settled (Flashbang, hinder version). Ours: Flashbang as above; no Magnetic Grenade anywhere.
2. **Sharpshooter wording:** BZ and W's text say 0.75 % of damage dealt (seconds per damage, W's table 140 x 0.0075 = 1.05 s); W's
   infobox field says "of base cooldown" (0.0375 s of a 5 s roll per crit: too small to matter, likely a slip). Ours: 0.0075 s per point
   of crit damage dealt (falloff-reduced damage counts as dealt).
3. **Primary falloff end:** BZ 2025-03-25 names only the start (20 -> 25 m); W says 25-35, keeping the 10 m span the 2024-07-09
   rescale had. Ours: 25-35 m, floor 30 %, linear.
4. **Fan the Hammer radius:** W 0.04 (large), though BZ 2024-02-13's rules would class a spread shot as small (0.02 now). Ours: 0.04.
5. **Flashbang projectile:** speed 30 m/s is flagged on W as an unverified old value; the 0.2 m radius is not split into base + global;
   arc gravity unknown. Ours: 30 m/s, 0.2 m, 7 m range.
6. **Deadeye:** the "It's high noon" and "Draw" voice timing, ult charge gained during the channel, the exact fade curve over 1.4 s
   (linear assumed), what "warning time" shows to enemies: not in the pages read.
7. **Combat Roll:** speed profile across the 0.4 s (constant 15 m/s assumed), the exact reload moment, and whether the -50 % covers the
   whole 0.416 s.
8. **Giddy Up decay curve** and whether it stacks with other speed boosts: no source.
9. **Reload timing:** "1.5 s animation, ammo after 0.8 s" (W) is the same ambiguous form as Tracer's and Pharah's.
10. **Ult cost 1800, perk XP 1560 / 3900** and the hit volume units are W / BZ only; a game-data pass would settle all three.
