# Kiriko vs real Overwatch: picky visual critique (2026-10-26)

Our screenshots in `run/screenshots/` compared with the owner's gameplay photos (Aspen's videos, May 2026 build) and
`kiriko-reference.md` / `kiriko-effects.md`. Text only (repo rule). The side-by-side crops used for the measurements
are in the session scratchpad (`...\scratchpad\critique\pair_*.png`, `ref_queued_0N.png` = the phone photos
normalised to 1920x1080). They hold Overwatch frames, so they stay out of the repo.

**How it was measured:** each reference photo was cropped to its video frame and scaled to 1920x1080. Our captures
were cropped at the same coordinates, and sizes come from white-pixel bounding boxes. The reference is blurred, so its
boxes run about 1-2 px small. All pixel values are 1080p.

**Confidence:** H = measured or clearly seen in both; M = seen once, or partly inferred; L = general knowledge, or
seen poorly.

**Tags:** **[BROKEN]** looks like a bug, **[EXTRA]** something ours shows that Overwatch doesn't, **[MISSING]**
something Overwatch has that ours lacks.

Not counted as findings, per the brief: the flat test world, the villager standing in as the ally, Minecraft's sky,
test-command chat lines, 250 HP and Minecraft mode's small ult meter.

## Ranked list (most noticeable first)

### 1. Healing Ofuda locked on an ally: giant lime glow balls [BROKEN] (H)
- **Where:** effect, every heal, in the middle of the screen.
- **Overwatch:** the talisman itself glows gold and leaves a thin yellow trail. In the owner's frame (`crops/center.png`, around the ally's red "+") the trails are thin streaks about 40-60 px long and 3-4 px wide. Colour ramp: #A18700 / #DCB900 / white-gold (`kiriko-effects.md`, Healing Ofuda 7E2D).
- **Ours:** `fx2_sheet_a.png`, tiles 1-2: each ofuda becomes a soft glow sphere about 450 px across at 1080p, sampled lime (#B7C238, #C8D159, core #FFFF92). A yellow capsule sits inside it, and Minecraft-style square white particles scatter around. Tile 2 looks like two yellow lightsabers. The ally reads as tinted yellow.
- **Fix:**
  - Draw the talisman as the paper quad (0.25 x 0.75 m, white with the red seal).
  - Put a gold glow quad of about 1 m behind it (data: FFC9, size 1). On screen that is about 30-60 px at a typical range, not 450.
  - Add a ribbon trail of about 0.2 m.
  - Additive gold over green grass turns lime. Mix in some alpha blending, or pre-multiply toward #DCB900 so it stays gold.
  - Replace the square white particles with the 5-petal flower (0.32 m, 0.34 s) on the hit.
