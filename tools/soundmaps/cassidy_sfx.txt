# 01B with their node numbers. Confidence: H read in the data, M inferred, L guess. Stadium powers' sets are left out.
# Gains: Wwise volume + make-up gain summed from each file up through its containers and mixers (the middle of a random
# range included) plus its output bus chain, plus RTPC curves (dB scaling 20*log10(1+y); linear between points) and
# SetVolume actions. RTPCs are read at their game parameter's default (the Init bank's list); modulators: an LFO at its
# middle, a time modulator at its start on one-shots and at its end on loops. As in sojourn.txt, ONE offset per family keeps
# his sounds' balance with each other, bringing the family's loudest single layer to -1 dBFS (the builder's limiter catches
# sums): own -0.5 dB (loudest: cassidy.peacekeeper.hit_enemy), 3p +0.5 dB (loudest: cassidy.peacekeeper.hit_enemy_3p).
# Pitch: cents as a speed factor (*). Low / high-pass filters, random pitch / volume per play, fades, Seek, Wwise states
# and the voice priority RTPCs are not reproduced. Layers more than 60 dB down are left out, and in loops those 20 dB
# under the loudest loop. A loop's LOOP LENGTH is its longest bed within 6 dB of the loudest layer; shorter layers are
# tiled across it and beds more than 15 % longer are left out.
# Own vs others: audience-1 copies and no-range copies are the player the effect plays on ('own': Cassidy, or the shooter for
# a hit, M as in Sigma's map); ranged copies are everyone else's ('3p'); where two ranged copies hold the same files the
# louder is built (L which team's / near or far). A sound with no own copy is built once for everyone ('both').
